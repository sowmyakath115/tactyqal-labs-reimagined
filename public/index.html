GOOD TO KNOW

Questions from parents.

Get the details before beginning your journey.

Does my teen need coding experience?

No. The program supports beginners with guided, no-code development.

How much time should we plan for?

The original program describes around two to three hours each week, including a mentoring session and independent practice.

What kinds of products can students build?

Projects can include commerce, education, AI tools and products that address social problems.

How can we learn more about enrollment?

Contact the Tactyqal team for current availability, fees and program details.
